# China Business Associates (CBA)

**China Business Associates, Inc. (CBA)** is a pioneer in U.S.–China bilateral trade, cross-border investment, and high-tech manufacturing. Established in California in 1980, CBA was the first permanent American company registered in the People's Republic of China following diplomatic normalization.

---

## Highlights

- **Historic First:** First permanent American enterprise registered in Beijing (1980), leading bilateral trade delegations and joint ventures.
- **Strategic Advisory:** Over 40 years advising Fortune 500 corporations and tech pioneers—including IBM, Motorola, and Novell—on China market entry, regulatory strategy, and partnerships.
- **High-Tech Manufacturing:** Established **U-Tron (Beijing) Electronics Co., Ltd.** in 1992 as a Wholly Owned Foreign Enterprise (WOFE) in Beijing Economic-Technological Development Area (BDA Yizhuang).
- **Infrastructure & Innovation:** Pioneer in Intelligent Transportation Systems (ITS), industrial automation, and high-power municipal LED lighting solutions.
- **Olympic Infrastructure:** Provided high-power LED installations and transit systems for the 2008 Beijing Olympic Games.
- **Bilingual & Transpacific Network:** Executive presence and facilities bridging Silicon Valley, Beijing, and global partner markets.
